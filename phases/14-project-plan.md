# §14 Project plan (16 weeks)

Four monthly milestones; demo on staging + written acceptance each month.

| Milestone | When | Summary |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **M1** | End month 1 | Requirements/UI; arch & DB; env & pipeline; accounts, KYC, catalog |
| **M2** | End month 2 | Listings, search, cart/checkout, gateway on staging |
| **M3** | End month 3 | Auctions, offers, orders/shipping, ledger/escrow/payouts, messaging/feedback |
| **M4** | End month 4 | Returns/disputes, seller + admin, test/security/perf, UAT, go-live, training |

**Team (proposal):** PM (PT), tech lead/architect, 2 backend, 1 frontend, UX (PT), QA, DevOps (PT).

**Cadence:** weekly progress call; **biweekly demo**.
